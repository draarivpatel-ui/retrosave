RETROSAVE v1.0 / CRT EDITION

A dungeon you can replay frame by frame.

Twelve procedurally generated CRT floors. Boss rush every third floor. Deterministic seeds, a frame-accurate speedrun clock, and replays you can copy, paste, and verify. Zero dependencies, one canvas, all keyboard and gamepad.

Same seed plus same inputs equals the exact same run. Try a friend's seed and race their ghost.

Local High Scores

Runs are stored in this browser as checksummed records. Nothing leaves your machine.

Replay Lab

A replay is a seed plus a compressed list of input changes. Paste one below to watch it play back from frame zero, or copy your most recent run and share it.

Records are checksummed with FNV-1a. A single corrupted character invalidates the replay.

Sprite Editor

Paint an 8x8 bitmask. Each row is one byte, written as two hex digits. Export a JSON sprite sheet for your own projects.
